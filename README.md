# The Small Hours

Painted stories about the next few years of AI, by Jeff Kazzee. Every frame is drawn in code with Canvas 2D. No image generators were used.

Live at https://jeff-kazzee.github.io/the-small-hours/

| Story | What it is |
|---|---|
| [Ten Thousand Windows](ten-thousand-windows/) | An interactive story with seven choices and nine endings. The research behind it is linked inside. |
| [Finite Time](finite-time/) | A short film. A father tells his daughter a bedtime story about a river that could think. |
| [Hand Over Hand](hand-over-hand/) | A music video about a parent at a kitchen table at 3 a.m. |

The labs in these stories are fictional.
